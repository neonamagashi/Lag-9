# Lag 9 (2.5D prototype)

A small playable room for the 2.5D roguelite direction: the Blue Witch in a ¾-view dungeon arena with the gravity kit, against three waves of monsters, plus a mimic hiding as a chest.

Open this folder (`lag9-2d/`) as its own project in Godot 4.7 and press F5. The 3D project in the repo root ignores this folder.

## Controls
| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| WASD | Move |
| Mouse | Aim |
| LMB (hold) | Pierce: teal orb bolts |
| RMB (hold, release) | Send: charged cone blast; enemies slammed into walls, pillars or each other take impact damage |
| E | Draw: yank enemies toward you (into your field) |
| R | Rend: ice spikes erupt under the 5 nearest enemies |
| Space | Snap: dash with true i-frames |
| Q | Flip the field between Crush and Lift |
| G | Singularity (60 mana): field grows 2.4x, enemies in it slow to 35%, crush doubles |
| 1 | Drink whisky (heal) |
| F1 | Show/hide controls |
| F5 | Restart |

## The gravity field
- **Crush (violet):** enemies inside slow down and take damage over time (more in the inner ring). Light ones (rats, bats) are crushed fast; slimes slowly; the mimic barely. Flying bats are slammed to the ground and stunned.
- **Lift (gold):** light and medium enemies float up helplessly above their shadows. Flip back to Crush while they're up to slam them down for big damage.

Tuning numbers sit at the top of `scripts/witch.gd` (abilities) and `scripts/enemy.gd` (enemy stats, field damage per weight).

## Layout
- `assets/witch`, `assets/monsters`: the sprite packs, untouched.
- `assets/env`: placeholder dungeon art generated by `tools/gen_env.py` (swap in a real tileset later).
- `scripts/`: `main.gd` (room, waves), `witch.gd`, `enemy.gd`, `projectile.gd`, `pickup.gd`, `fx.gd`, `hud.gd`.
- `tests/play_test.tscn`: headless check of every mechanic plus a bot playing all three waves. `tests/shots.tscn` renders screenshots.
